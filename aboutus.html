About Us

Learn more about Brew Haven

Our Story

Founded in 2022, Brew Haven began as a simple idea from a creative graduate. He took his love for coffee and turned it into a business.

Our Mission

We aim to create a safe, relaxing and welcoming environment for all our customers.

Our Staff

Our baristas and staff are the heart of Brew Haven. Each is trained personally by the owner who has 4 years of experience making intricate coffee designs and delicious baked goods.

Why Choose Us

Whether you need a warm morning coffee or a freshly made muffin, Brew Haven is the perfect place to stop. Our aesthetic café-style shop is perfect for your next social media post.
